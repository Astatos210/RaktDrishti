Affordable Point-of-Care Hemoglobin Screening

Fast, Affordable Anemia Screening — When It Matters Most.

Raktdrishti is a student-built hemoglobin testing prototype designed to make anemia screening quicker, more affordable, and easier to access. Using a small blood sample of approximately 10–15 mL, it helps deliver rapid readings without depending on a full laboratory setup.

The problem

Anemia Often Goes Undetected.

Anemia can affect energy, concentration, pregnancy outcomes, and overall well-being. Yet testing is often delayed because laboratory testing may be expensive, time-consuming, or difficult to access.

01

Delayed Testing

Traditional testing can require travel, sample handling, and waiting for laboratory reports.

02

Limited Accessibility

Remote communities and health camps may not always have convenient access to laboratory infrastructure.

03

Cost Barriers

Repeated screening can become difficult when affordable testing options are unavailable.

The solution

Introducing Raktdrishti

Raktdrishti is a compact anemia-screening prototype that aims to make hemoglobin testing more accessible. It combines a small-sample blood testing approach with a digital interface to provide rapid, easy-to-understand results.

  • Small sample requirement: approximately 0.07–0.1 mL
  • Faster workflow than conventional lab-based testing
  • Designed with affordability in mind
  • Portable concept for screening environments

How it works

Simple Testing. Clear Results.

A clear, prototype-level workflow designed for screening contexts and professional review.

01

Collect Sample

A small blood sample of approximately 10–15 mL is collected following appropriate safety procedures.

02

Insert for Analysis

The sample is placed in the Raktdrishti testing setup for measurement.

03

Process Reading

The device processes the sample and estimates hemoglobin-related readings.

04

View Result

Results are displayed digitally for quick screening and further professional review if required.
